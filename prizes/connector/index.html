The Everyday Prize List connector for Claude

Last updated: November 5, 2026

What it does

Ask Claude which sweepstakes you can enter today. The connector gives Claude the biggest prizes on today’s Everyday Prize List, up to 10, with each sponsor’s own entry link. The full list comes free by email every day.

How to add it

In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors. Choose Add custom connector and paste this address: https://www.coffeeshopoffice.com/mcp. No account or sign-in is needed.

Tools

Today’s top sweepstakes (get_todays_top_sweepstakes): returns up to 10 of today’s biggest sweepstakes with prize value, sponsor, a 21+ flag and the entry link. Read only.

Get the full daily list (get_full_list_signup): returns the signup link for the free daily email. Read only.

Example prompts

“What sweepstakes can I enter today?” “Show me today’s three biggest giveaways.” “How do I get the full prize list?”

Good to know

Entering a sweepstakes is always your choice. Check each sweepstakes’ official rules before you enter. The connector does not enter sweepstakes for you.

Privacy

The connector does not ask for or store any personal information. See the Privacy Policy.
